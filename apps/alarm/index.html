Before you buy

Your phone already does this

  1. Clock has alarms. Clock › Alarms: a time, the days it repeats, a label, a sound and snooze.
  2. A different wake-up time each day. Clock’s Sleep | Wake Up alarm, set up in Health › Sleep, wakes you at a different time on each day of the week.
  3. Reminders and Calendar repeat. For a nudge rather than an alarm, a repeating reminder or event will alert you.

If that’s all you need, keep your seven dollars.

What we add

  • Time loops. Several times on different days in one routine: 7:00 on Monday and Friday, noon and 2:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Or every 30 minutes from 9 to 5. In Clock that’s a pile of separate alarms.
  • Wake-up missions. Eight optional challenges before the alarm counts as done: sums, typing a phrase, scanning a code you keep in another room, memory tiles, shaking, walking and more.
  • A wake-up check. One follow-up alarm after you get up, in case you went back to bed.
  • Naps and groups. One-tap naps from 15 minutes to an hour, groups you switch on and off together, and defaults for new alarms.
  • A history of your mornings. Every dismissal and finished mission, searchable, with CSV and backup export.
