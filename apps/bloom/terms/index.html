Terms preview

Last updated 16 November 2026. Every change is in the site’s public history.

Not on sale yet

Bloom is in planning. No download, trial or purchase is offered here. Features and release timing are still being determined.

The purchase plan

Seven days to try the app, then $7 once for the unlock. No automatic charge or subscription. The store will show the exact local price before purchase. Apple family unlock is planned; Google Play unlock is per account.

Your records

Our commitment is that existing records remain readable and exportable after the trial. Sharing a purchase will not share health or intimate records.

Tracking and reminders

Bloom is planned as a personal tracking and reminder tool. It is not a contraceptive method or a diagnosis. Any cycle estimates will be labelled as estimates; a reminder is not proof that a dose was taken.

Before release

We will publish the final feature list, platform requirements, purchase terms and privacy details before the app becomes available. This page describes the plan and does not offer features for sale.
