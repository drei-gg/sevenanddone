Before you buy

Your phone already does this

  1. Photos finds exact duplicates. Open Photos, scroll down to Utilities, then tap Duplicates. Merge keeps the best copy and moves the rest to Recently Deleted.
  2. Contacts finds doubled-up cards. When it spots some, a Duplicates Found card sits at the top of your contacts list. Tap it to view and merge them.
  3. Settings shows what’s taking space. Settings › General › iPhone Storage lists the biggest apps and suggests what to clear, such as large videos.

If that’s all you need, keep your seven dollars.

What we add

  • More than exact copies. It tells exact copies apart from re-saved versions and from shots that are merely similar, and says why each group was matched.
  • You decide, at your pace. Mark each photo or video keep, later or remove. Your marks survive closing the app, and new scans never overwrite them. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and deletions go through Photos’ own Recently Deleted.
  • Biggest first. Photos and videos sorted by their real size, with playback and zoom right in the review.
  • Smaller copies, originals kept. Shrink ordinary photos into new copies, checked for detail and their date and place. The original stays where it was.
  • Contacts compared field by field. See which cards look like the same person and why, side by side, and save a copy of them first.
  • Folders you choose. Pick a folder in Files to see what’s big in it and find exact duplicate files.
