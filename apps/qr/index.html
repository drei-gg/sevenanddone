Before you buy

Your phone already does this

  1. The Camera reads QR codes. Point it at a code and tap the link that appears.
  2. Code Scanner, in Control Center. Add it to Control Center once, then tap it to scan.
  3. Photos reads codes in pictures. Open the photo, tap the Live Text button, then tap the code.

If that’s all you need, keep your seven dollars.

What we add

  • Where a code really goes. Every scan is checked on your phone for lookalike addresses, disguised links, raw IP addresses and other scam signs, with the reasons spelled out. The Camera opens whatever it finds.
  • Who a payment code pays. Merchant payment codes, UPI, SEPA and Swiss QR-bills are read before your banking app opens: the name, amount, currency and checksum.
  • Peek. One tap follows the link’s redirects, shows where it ends up, spots password and card-number forms, and asks how old the domain is, without running the page’s code.
  • Codes from anything. Links, Wi-Fi, contacts, events and more, from the app or any share sheet. Export PNG, SVG or a PDF ready to print.
  • History. What you scanned and made, searchable, with JSON and CSV export.
