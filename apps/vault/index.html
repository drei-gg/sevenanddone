Before you buy

Your phone already does this

  1. Photos already hides and locks pictures. Touch and hold a photo, choose Hide, then confirm. Find it in Hidden under Utilities, protected by your device authentication. Apple’s guide ↗
  2. Notes can lock supported notes. Open a note, tap More, then Lock. Choose your device passcode or a separate Notes password. Apple’s guide ↗

If that’s all you need, keep your seven dollars.

What we add

  • Different secrets, different vaults. A PIN, drawn pattern or passphrase opens its matching encrypted library. The locked screen does not list your vault names.
  • More than photos. Keep videos, documents and notes together, with folders, favourites, search and batch import. Imports leave the originals alone.
  • Backups you control. Save an encrypted backup or choose a Files folder for automatic copies. Keep the recovery key separately. Cloud-folder delivery depends on your provider and iOS; this is backup, not live sync.
  • Your way out stays free. Read, organise, export, back up and restore existing content after the trial. Your files never become a reason to pay again.

Keep your recovery key somewhere safe, separately from an encrypted backup. Losing both your secret and recovery key loses access; we cannot reset them for you. Importing never erases the source. Copies you export are outside Vault’s protection.
