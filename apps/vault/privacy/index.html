7&D Vault

Privacy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Every change is in the site’s public history.

The short version

Vault has no account, ads, analytics or crash-reporting service. We run no server for your vaults and do not receive your files or secrets. Encrypted libraries stay on your device; backups and exports go to the destinations you choose.

Who we are

7&D Vault is made by Drei Guintu under the Seven and Done name. Questions: support@sevenanddone.app.

What stays on your device

  • Your encrypted libraries. Imported files, photos, videos and notes, plus names, folders, favourites and trash state. Different credentials open separate vaults.
  • Access settings. Encrypted key information, recovery configuration and optional biometric-unlock material protected by the system Keychain. Biometric matching is handled by iOS; Vault does not receive your fingerprint or face data.
  • Backup state. Your selected folder access, pending encrypted copies and delivery status.
  • Trial and purchase state. Local trial settings and Apple’s StoreKit entitlement information.

Deleting an item moves it to Vault’s trash; permanent deletion asks for confirmation. Removing the app removes its local libraries. Backups and exported copies elsewhere are separate and must be removed at their destinations.

Previews and exports

Opening or exporting content creates temporary unencrypted material for system viewers and sharing. Vault clears its own temporary files when locking, dismissing previews and on next launch. Once you save or share a copy outside Vault, that destination controls it. Importing does not delete the original from Photos or Files.

When data leaves the device

WhenWhat goes where
You import cloud-backed media or filesiOS may download the selected content from Apple or your chosen Files provider.
You save or enable automatic backups to a cloud folderEncrypted backup files go to the Files provider you select. Automatic delivery may retry in the background. Copying to a provider does not prove a remote upload has finished.
You export or shareThe selected unencrypted originals go to the app, person or destination you choose.
You buy or restore a purchaseApple handles the transaction and entitlement.
You email supportWe receive your email address and whatever you include. Do not send your secret or recovery key.

These providers operate under their own policies. Your device-backup settings may also include Vault’s local data. We do not operate a cloud-storage service or receive your backup keys.

Recovery and deletion

Keep a recovery key and an encrypted backup separately. We cannot recover missing files or unlock a library when both its secret and recovery key are lost. Turning off automatic backup leaves existing destination copies in place.

Changes

The date above changes when this policy changes. Earlier versions remain in the site’s public history.
