Before you buy

Your phone already does this

  1. Notes scans documents. In a note, tap the camera button, then Scan Documents. It finds the edges, takes several pages and saves a PDF.
  2. So does Files. In Files, tap the More button, then Scan Documents, to save a scan straight into a folder.
  3. Live Text reads the words. Text in a scan or a photo can be selected, copied and looked up.

If that’s all you need, keep your seven dollars.

What we add

  • One library for your paper. Every document in one place, searchable by its name, its note and the words on its pages.
  • Searchable PDFs, your way. Export all pages or some, on A4 or US Letter, at full or reduced size, with an invisible text layer so the PDF can be searched and copied from.
  • Fix a page without losing it. Rotate, brighten, add contrast or go grayscale. The original stays underneath, and Reset brings it back.
  • Bring in what you have. Import images and PDFs from Files, or add new pages to an old document.
  • A safety net and a backup. Deleted documents wait in Recently Deleted until you say otherwise, and one backup file carries your whole library anywhere in Files.
