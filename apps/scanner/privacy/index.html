7&D Document Scanner

Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026. Every change is in the site’s public history.

The short version

We collect nothing. Scanner has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporter, and we run no server for it to call. What you make in the app stays on your phone until you delete it. Apple’s privacy label for it reads Data Not Collected.

Who we are

7&D Document Scanner is made and sold by Drei Guintu under the Seven and Done ($7&D) name. Questions about this policy go to support@sevenanddone.app.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these in its own storage on your iPhone. We can’t see them. They are included in your iPhone backups the way any app’s files are, under your Apple account’s settings, not ours.

  • Your documents. The original page images, your adjustments, the recognized text, names, notes and favorites. A deleted document goes to Recently Deleted; delete it there and it’s gone.
  • Exports in progress. PDFs being prepared for sharing. They’re removed automatically after 24 hours.
  • Your trial and purchase status. Kept by Apple’s StoreKit, so the unlock follows your Apple account.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Backups go wherever you save them in Files. They aren’t encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

What the app asks to use

iOS asks you first, and you can change your answer in Settings at any time. Saying no only switches off the feature that needs it.

PermissionUsed for
CameraScanning paper. Asked the first time you scan.
FilesNot asked. You pick each file to import, and where each export or backup goes.

When the app goes online

Only when a feature you tap needs it. Nothing goes to us: these requests go straight from your phone to the place named, and they carry no account, cookie or identifier of ours.

Scanning and text recognition happen entirely on your phone.

Like any connection, those places see your IP address. iOS’s own App Privacy Report (Settings › Privacy & Security) lists every connection the app makes, so you can check this yourself.

Buying it

The trial and the $7 unlock go through Apple. Apple handles your payment details; we never see them. Apple sends us sales totals and the country of each sale, never who you are.

If you write to us

We see your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it to reply and for nothing else. We don’t add you to a list. Ask and we’ll delete the thread.

No one else

The app contains no third-party code that collects anything: no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting kits. We don’t sell, share or rent data, because we don’t have any.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Wherever you live (the EU and UK’s GDPR, California’s CCPA, the Philippines’ Data Privacy Act and the rest), you can ask what we hold about you and have it deleted. For the app the answer is nothing. For an email thread, write to us and it’s gone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and the old wording stays in the public history. If an update ever adds a kind of network contact, it appears here and in the app before it ships.
