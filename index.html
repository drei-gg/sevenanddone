Stop renting
solved apps.

Simple, useful tools shouldn’t come with a recurring bill.

#$7&D! (Seven and Done) is a shelf of small apps. Each one replaces something that bills you every week for a job your phone mostly does already. Ours cost $7. Once. You own it, you use it, you move on.

  • $7. Done.

    That’s the whole price, for the whole family.

  • No servers.

    We don’t run a backend for your phone to call.

  • Built fast.

    About seven engineering days each. Small on purpose.

  • Your phone may already do it.

    We’ll say so up front. Then you keep your $7.

  • Source available.

    GPL-3.0. Build it yourself if you’d rather.

  • Nothing renews.

    Pay once. Use it. Move on.

The apps

Practical utilities. Fixed price. No subscriptions.

  1. No. 1

    #$7&D!QR

    Building

    Read and make QR codes. Warns you about scams, for free.

    Before you buy: Your phone’s camera already reads QR codes. We add the scam check, payment-code reading and a code maker.

    Details, privacy, terms →
  2. No. 2

    #$7&D!Scanner

    Building

    Pages to clean, searchable PDFs, kept in a library on your phone.

    Before you buy: Notes and Files on your phone already scan documents to PDF. We add the library, page fixes and searchable exports.

    Details, privacy, terms →
  3. No. 3

    #$7&D!Alarm

    Building

    Alarms that make you get up, and loops that repeat on your schedule.

    Before you buy: Clock already has alarms that repeat by weekday. We add time loops, wake-up missions and a history.

    Details, privacy, terms →
  4. No. 4

    #$7&D!Fast

    Building

    A private fasting timer with a history worth keeping.

    Before you buy: Clock times things, Notes keeps a journal and Health records weight. We put them in one place, with a calendar.

    Details, privacy, terms →
  5. No. 5

    #$7&D!Cleaner

    Vetting

    Clear out duplicate photos, near-duplicates and giant videos.

    Before you buy: Photos on your phone already has a Duplicates album. We’ll say what we add before you pay.
