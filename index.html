Seven
and Done.

Simple apps, paid for once.

Somewhere right now, someone is paying $7.99 a week to delete duplicate photos from their own phone. That’s $415 a year, for work their phone does by itself.

We think that’s absurd. So here’s the deal.

The Sevens

And the fine print, in the same size type.

  1. 7

    apps

    We start with seven small apps, each one replacing something that charges you rent.

    Fine print

    Seven is where we start, not where we stop. The line grows for as long as there are subscriptions worth retiring.

  2. 7

    engineering days

    Each app is scoped to about seven days of engineering. Small on purpose, and done when it does its job.

    Fine print

    It’s a measure of effort, not a stopwatch. One app takes three, another takes ten. Seven is the budget we scope to, and the build log says when we go over.

  3. 7

    day trial

    Use the whole app free for a week. When the week ends, it asks. It never bills you on its own.

    Fine print

    The clock starts when you install. There’s no sign-up to start it, no card up front, and no charge at the end you didn’t choose.

  4. $7

    once

    Pay once, keep it. No subscriptions, no weekly fees, no “Pro” tier waiting behind the paywall.

    Fine print

    $7 is the US price. Where seven of your currency is worth more, it’s that: €7, £7. Where $7 is a lot of money, it costs less.

  5. 7

    month updates

    Every app gets at least one update every seven months, for as long as we’re around.

    Fine print

    The cycle is for chores: new OS versions, toolchain updates, the dull work that keeps an app alive. Critical bugs and security fixes ship when they’re found, not when the calendar says. New features are a bonus, and supporters can help fund them. Whatever they fund ships to everyone who owns the app.

Done means done

Each app does its one job well, then stops growing. Here’s what none of them will ever have.

  • No subscriptions. Not weekly, not yearly, not “optional”.
  • No ads. You paid. That’s the business model.
  • No accounts. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
  • No servers. Everything runs on your phone, so there’s nothing for us to bill you monthly for.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no third-party code. The apps collect nothing.
  • No second paywall. $7 buys the whole app, not the right to see the next price.
